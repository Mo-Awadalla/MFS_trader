The historical long-history adjusted Yahoo sample had 5,405 bars, 258 rebalances, 78 walk-forward folds and a DSR p-value of 3.48 × 10⁻⁷. Strategy CAGR was 7.89% versus 10.86% for SPY, annual volatility was 10.20% versus 18.96%, and maximum drawdown was 17.30% versus 55.19%.
