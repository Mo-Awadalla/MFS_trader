BROAD NATIVE FUNDING SCREEN — NO QUALIFIED HFT CANDIDATE
Status: independently audited feasibility screen. No strategy P&L was run.

We checked the first day of every month in 2024, immediately before the
08:00 and 16:00 UTC funding times. Historical native Binance messages
provided the universe, avoiding a present-day list of surviving contracts.
This is a dependent extension of the previous six-contract funding study.

Scope and cost hurdle
  24 full-channel native slices; 348 distinct USDT symbols;
  6,552 symbol/event states. Decisions use only messages received before
  funding minus 30 seconds. Both capture and publication must be within
  five seconds, and the advertised next funding time must match the event.
  E is a publication timestamp, not a book transaction timestamp.

  Eighteen observations had an absolute Binance rate of at least 14 bp.
  Their 16 distinct symbol/date counterparts were requested from Bybit.
  Fifteen files were available, containing 1,419,702 normalized updates.
  The proposed equal-unit payment difference had to exceed 28 bp:
  four executions, each charged 6 bp plus 1 bp adverse allowance.
  Spreads and basis movement would still need to be paid after this screen.

Results after lifecycle correction
  14 observations: payment difference below 28 bp.
   2 observations: Binance had prohibited new positions and settled/delisted
                   the contract earlier that day (DGBUSDT and CTKUSDT).
   1 observation: Bybit source was older than the frozen five-second limit.
   1 observation: requested FETUSDT counterpart source unavailable.
   0 observations: qualified for execution replay.

The tempting false positive
  DGBUSDT at 2024-04-01 16:00 UTC showed a nominal 109.88941749 bp payment
  difference. Binance's mark-price stream still had fresh event and capture
  timestamps. However, its dated notice prohibited new positions from
  08:30 UTC and settled/delisted the contract at 09:00 UTC. The proposed
  entry at 15:59:30 was impossible. Fresh feed timestamps did not establish
  that the instrument was tradable. CTKUSDT was covered by the same notice.

  Primary notice, published March 25, 2024:
  https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/ce74360a3fd444b2b35da7186f8dd0ad

FET was not silently deleted or treated as a zero return
  The provider returned HTTP 400 / code 140 and explicitly states its
  Bybit FETUSDT coverage ends at 2024-06-08 00:00 UTC, exclusive, identifying
  a delisted instrument. The response is saved. The exchange notice body
  was inaccessible; the original unknown-counterpart status is retained.

  Binance's separate merger notice was updated on June 20 to retain FET
  trading. Its old proposed FET delisting must not be applied to July 1.
  https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/e485541c370240adba3929fded74f269

Audit and preservation
  Independent code verified all 24 native source hashes and decoded hashes,
  examined 428,520 native messages, reconstructed all 6,552 as-of states,
  and checked all 15 available Bybit files and 18 paired dispositions.
  Maximum independent payment-gap error: 1.25e-14 bp.
  Native acquisition: 91,925,162 decoded bytes / 17,129,667 compressed bytes.
  Successful counterpart acquisition: 17,125,325 compressed bytes.
  Five focused timing/state tests passed; new audit scripts passed Ruff.

  Original stage_a_results.json, stage_b_results.json and funding_pairs.parquet
  are preserved. lifecycle_facts.json records the correction and retrieval
  limits. funding_pairs_lifecycle_v1.parquet applies it additively.
  verification.json records the independent checks and hashes.

Limits and decision
  This is a large-Binance-rate screen; it does not cover Bybit-only extreme
  rates when Binance is below the 14 bp prescreen. It is not an exhaustive
  proof that funding HFT cannot work. The data are monthly sample days.
  Bybit native snapshot/delta normalization, complete instrument histories,
  historical order acceptance and contract unit equivalence were not proven.
  No filled orders, realized funding cashflows or profitable strategy are
  claimed. No execution replay is warranted for the present candidates.
  Economic strategy counts remain unchanged: 120 configurations / 170
  implementation versions across the search. No reliable edge established.
