POST-VOLATILITY-HALT STOCK HFT FEASIBILITY -- 2026-09-30

VERDICT: NO QUALIFYING ORDER IN THE FROZEN DEVELOPMENT COHORT.
This is a feasibility result, not a measured trading loss or evidence that
post-halt HFT can never work. No entry or exit profitability was evaluated.
No reliable edge, actual execution or competition win has been established.

DESIGN
Investigate long upside continuation and long downside rebound after a
volatility pause. Each has a planned 15s and 30s hold, four configurations.
Entry is after a reported reopening and five seconds of observed price
support. The reopening auction price is a signal anchor, never an assumed
entry fill. Costs, guarded FOK entry, acknowledgement/outgoing delays, quote
ambiguity, repeat-halt inventory and progression gates are frozen in
specification.json before quote/trade outcomes were opened.

The free NYSE archive contained 73,832 records, including 27,403 LULD pauses
in 2023-25. Historical names and symbols, including subsequently delisted
ones, define a fixed common/ordinary-share cohort. Select the first LULD
halt per symbol on the first NYSE trading session of each month in 2023-24,
then apply the predeclared clock and 295-900 second same-day duration filters.
The first halt is never replaced with a later one when it fails a filter.
152 events: 78 development events in 2023; 74 reserved events in 2024.
Only 2023 quotes/trades were acquired. 2024 remains unopened for selection;
no 2025/2026 economic outcomes were used in this family.

SIGNAL
The prehalt midpoint move from halt-30s to halt-1s must be >=100bp for upside
continuation or <=-100bp for downside rebound. Require valid latest strictly
prior quotes, at most five seconds old. A primary-venue reopening trade
must carry condition 5, have positive price/size, contain no specified late
or special-price flags, and align within five seconds of the archive resume.
Both rules require reopening price at least 50bp above the last prehalt
midpoint, then a bid at/above reopening price after five seconds. The decision
quote must be post-reopening, at most one second old relative to the modeled
source cutoff, with ask $2-$500 and spread <=50bp. Fixed causal quantity is
<=10% observed ask depth and <=$10,000 cash, with a $100 minimum ticket.

Execution plans include $0.0075/share, minimum $1 commission each side;
sell 0.30bp plus $0.0002/share; 1bp adverse allowance each side; one-second
outgoing/return delay plus 250ms processing. Stress double fees, double
adverse allowance, three-second delay and +/-100ms quote alignment. Since
no signal reaches sizing/entry, these execution plans were not evaluated.

SOURCE RECOVERY AND IMPLEMENTATION CORRECTION
154 original files completed with full pagination: 145,043 trades and
90,743 quotes. Two trade requests initially failed the client's strict
list-schema check. The saved HTTP200 recovery bodies for GWII2023-02-01
and MSSA2023-07-03 explicitly contain trades:null and no next-page token.
They mean no provider records returned, not proof that no real trades took
place. Original failures, raw recovery bodies and hashes are preserved.

The original strict Nasdaq-Q implementation rejected 48 first-half2023
reopening prints labeled T in these files. Alpaca's official SDK/OpenAPI
identifies T as NASDAQ Int and Q as NASDAQ OMX; Nasdaq documents T as its
CTS market-center identifier. A versioned compatibility interpretation
allows both for Nasdaq-listed events. Native fields remain unchanged.
The exact provider normalization transition and identity of these Tape-C/T
reports have not been independently confirmed, so this compatibility screen
is explicitly conditional. No price/time/threshold/holding-period change
was made. Both original and corrected screens have zero eligible orders.

CORRECTED SCREEN, ALL 78 EVENTS
  53: missing, old, invalid or tied/ambiguous required signal quote.
  17: reopening concession or subsequent price support absent.
   6: no qualifying primary reopening print, including the two empty windows.
   2: price or spread gate fails.
   0: eligible order.

72 reopening anchors were identified: 48 original T codes and 24 Q codes.
Their source times are 0.008151154 to 0.995228536 seconds after the archived
resume second. This alignment supports the archive's time interpretation,
but does not establish message capture time or achievable trading latency.

Quote failures are not all missing data: some reflect the frozen freshness,
validity or ambiguity requirements. Nonexclusive failed quote counts are
33 prehalt, 34 thirty-second anchors and 38 decision quotes. No old valid
quote was substituted after a more recent invalid quote.

The 100-share historical lot multiplier is a stated conditional assumption
for this universe. Individual security definitions were not verified. It
does not affect this zero-order conclusion: all 78 events fail earlier
price/quote/anchor conditions. Do not claim the multiplier has been audited.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
Independent reconciliation checked every successful source hash, complete
pagination, 235,786 exact raw source timestamps, both empty-response bodies,
all 72 reopening anchors and all 78 feasibility dispositions. Latest quotes
were independently reconstructed using Boolean source-time masks; no exit
returns were opened. Nine focused tests pass, including qualifying synthetic
examples for both directions, future-quote invariance, strict cutoffs, ties,
invalid states, guarded FOK ambiguity, halt boundaries and exact fees.

This does not establish archive completeness, earliest administrative
publication, historical trade corrections/cancels as seen live, actual
provider receipt clocks, routing, queue position or real execution. It
establishes that the exact frozen screen makes no order on this sample under
either venue-code interpretation. Selection2024 is not opened to rescue it.
