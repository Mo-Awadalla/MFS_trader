Same-index ETF HFT pair screen: no qualifying supply
30 September 2026

No candidate advances to an economic replay. Across 21,600 frozen decision slots,
zero had an observable convergence budget meeting the cost and uncertainty
hurdle. Every one of the 17,606 sized cases had a negative budget even under
immediate full convergence to the causal reference. This is a feasibility
finding under the stated rules, not a realized loss, an execution backtest or
proof that every possible ETF-pair strategy fails. No reliable edge established.

The three fixed pairs were SPY/IVV (S&P 500), QQQ/QQQM (Nasdaq-100), and IWM/VTWO
(Russell 2000). These funds have related benchmark exposure; their shares are
not interchangeable and their NAVs, distributions and tracking can differ.
Mean reversion of their relative prices is a hypothesis, not riskless arbitrage.
The study belongs to the already-exposed cross-ETF research family. Different
securities and a paired payoff do not create statistical independence from the
broader search.

Frozen sample and calculation
-----------------------------
First XNYS trading session of each month in 2023: 3 Jan, 1 Feb, 1 Mar, 3 Apr,
1 May, 1 Jun, 3 Jul, 1 Aug, 1 Sep, 2 Oct, 1 Nov, 1 Dec. These dates were fixed
before market-data acquisition. All 72 historical SIP files completed, with
5,836,428 native quotes and 658,772,201 response bytes. No paid fallback, billing
change or live order was used.

Quotes were acquired for 09:30:00-09:35:20 Eastern. Each pair has 600 decisions
per date on a 250ms grid from 09:32:00 inclusive to 09:34:30 exclusive. A decision
uses only quotes strictly earlier than its time minus 100ms. This is an explicit
conditional source-delay assumption, not verified receipt latency. Historical
SIP access does not establish a free real-time consolidated feed.

The reference ratio is the exponentiated median log midpoint ratio of 60 causal
samples, at offsets 5-64 seconds before the current source cutoff. At least 45
joint valid samples are required. Dispersion is the median absolute log-ratio
deviation. No prior-day close, future observations or full-day calibration enter
the reference. Same-day calibration does not carry an unadjusted prior-day ratio
across the funds' potentially different distribution dates.

Quotes must have positive prices and sizes, be uncrossed and unlocked, and carry
regular R conditions and exchange fields. Current spreads must be <=20bp.
Conflicting same-timestamp states explicitly mean no order. Unchanged standing
quotes do not expire by age alone within the complete acquired prefix.

Each proposed book has $10,000 benchmark equity and <=$5,000/1.01 notional per
leg. Quantities are integers determined by known prices and <=10% of known
directional depth. Both legs must be >=$100. The hedge quantity follows the
causal ratio with <=2.5% unit mismatch. The highest common notional fitting both
legs is chosen. No aggregate shared-capital portfolio result is claimed.

Historical SIP sizes are treated as 100-share lots. This remains conditional
until instrument-definition confirmation; no actual fill or capacity is verified.
Short locates, availability and competition permission are also unverified.

Both orientations are evaluated. The hypothetical convergence point is the
geometric common price satisfying the reference ratio. Entry crosses the known
ask/bid; projected exit crosses current half-spreads around that reference.
The budget subtracts all four commissions ($0.0075/share, $1 minimum per order),
both sale charges (0.30bp plus $0.0002/share), 1bp adverse cost per execution,
and a full calendar day of short borrow at 5% annualized. Eligibility requires
remaining budget >= smaller-leg notional times (5bp + twice the log-ratio MAD).

This convergence budget is a conditional calculation, not expected realized
profit or a mathematical upper bound on future market movement. The screen
does not assume convergence actually occurs, fills are atomic, or a broker
provides these cost terms.

The best budgets are normalized by the smaller leg's entry notional, not by
total gross exposure or portfolio equity. They already include projected
convergence, entry/exit spreads and the assumed costs. They are not returns.

All 21,600 dispositions:
  17,606 sized cases below the cost and uncertainty hurdle
   3,174 missing, invalid or conflicting current states
     797 fewer than 45 valid historical reference samples
      23 no integer hedge satisfying notional, depth and mismatch constraints

The lack of qualifying supply is not solely a side effect of the positive
5bp uncertainty cushion: every sized budget is already negative before adding
that cushion. This does not make the invalid observations economically priced.
Their exclusions remain visible.

In particular, seven symbol/date windows have fewer than 300 valid current
states out of 600. Their dominant issue is conflicting tied timestamps:
QQQ on 3 Apr, 1 May and 1 Jun; VTWO on the same dates; QQQM on 1 Jun.
For example, QQQ has 452, 477 and 503 conflicting selected timestamp groups on
those three dates. All selected conditions there are R; this is not a change
to the regular-condition filter. The raw conflicting records remain intact.
No undocumented ordering within those timestamps is assumed.

The frozen supply gate requires at least 12 distinct proposed attempts across
six of the twelve dates, counted with a 60-second cooldown. All pairs fail.
The six proposed 15s/30s execution configurations were therefore not run.
There are zero new economic implementations from this screen. No future return
labels, controls, or 2024-25 selection results were evaluated.

Had supply passed, the planned replay would have needed separate leg receipts,
guarded limits, one-leg failure and unwind accounting, unresolved inventory,
250ms/1s latency stresses, wider timestamp bounds and doubled costs. None of
those unrun checks are represented as passed.

Verification
------------
Independent code reparsed native raw timestamps and checked all 72 source
hashes, ordered source intervals and completed pagination. It reconstructed
all 21,600 decisions using separate prefix lookup and statistics routines,
and independently calculated every 17,606 chosen convergence budget. Maximum
numeric difference was 3.82e-12. It confirmed zero hypothetical attempts.
The source-state diagnostic did not alter any frozen rule or open future P&L.

Seven focused tests pass: no invented profit at equal prices; correct
orientation for a relative gap; future-price/depth invariance; conservative
tied-state treatment; both-leg integer/depth sizing; all four costs plus
borrow; missing venue/size rejection. Test and audit success do not establish
investment performance. Ruff passes on the added modules.
