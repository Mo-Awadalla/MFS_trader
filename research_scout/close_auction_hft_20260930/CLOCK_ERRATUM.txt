HISTORICAL ARCX SCHEDULED AUCTION TIME -- 2026-09-30

The saved ARCX.PILLAR imbalance export used by this study has mixed encodings
of the scheduled auction_time field. Among 658 predecision SPY closing-auction
states, 619 contain the correct New York wall-clock hour encoded as UTC:
16:00 UTC rather than the actual 20:00 UTC (summer) or 21:00 UTC (winter).
39 states are already correct UTC. In this particular source, the observed
transition to correct encoding begins 2025-11-03. Databento's 2025-11-09
release note documents a NYSE auction_time UTC fix. Publication date and
observed source transition are different; do not use the publication date
as a repair cutoff.

Frozen repair: only a closing-auction record on a full 16:00 New York session
whose scheduled timestamp exactly equals that date's 16:00 encoded as UTC
is interpreted as New York wall time and localized using historical DST.
An already-correct scheduled UTC close is preserved. Other values are not
guessed. ts_recv, ts_event, signal decision times and all economic fields
are unchanged. This is a scheduled-time encoding repair, not a change to
message arrival or market-event clocks.

The original screen is preserved. The corrected screen admits 32 dates,
versus the original five. Subsequent conditional economic studies reject
both 15s and 30s configurations; the repair establishes no trading edge.

Scope: the exact local export and selected records inspected here. Do not
assume every export/provider/version has this encoding. The earlier opening
auction HFT forecast did not depend on scheduled auction_time, so this finding
does not by itself revise that forecast's economics. Older slow auction code
with a scheduled-clock filter requires its own review before reuse; no such
strategy was revived under the user's strictly-HFT requirement.

Independent verification checks all 658 mappings, non-schedule-field identity,
original native as-of source audit, and corrected cost/eligibility arithmetic.
See clock_corrected/specification.json and verification.json, and
correct_close_auction_clock_20260930.py for the frozen implementation.
