SPY CLOSING-AUCTION HFT RESEARCH -- 2026-09-30

VERDICT: NO QUALIFYING EDGE. Neither fixed 15-second nor 30-second rule
advances. No actual auction allocation or live execution has been verified.
All economic figures below assume full allocation when the official closing
price is within the precommitted LOC limit. They are conditional diagnostics,
not achieved trading returns, executable arbitrage, or physical P&L bounds.

HYPOTHESIS AND FROZEN DESIGN
Buy a price-protected closing-auction discount during a sell imbalance, then
sell seconds after a modeled auction-fill acknowledgement. This is related
to earlier auction research, with a different entry and seconds-long exit;
it is not independent evidence from an untouched strategy family.

658 full 16:00 New York sessions, 2023-03-28 through 2025-12-31, after the
previously identified ARCX/EQUS quality exclusions. All are exposed discovery
data. No 2026 outcomes were opened for selection. Decision: 15:58:55, using
strictly earlier quote and imbalance captures. Quote event/capture age <=250ms;
imbalance age <=2s, closing auction, valid status, not frozen, sell imbalance,
positive paired volume and valid prices. A frozen integer quantity uses
$10,000 reset cash, <=10% observed bid depth and <=1% paired auction volume,
with a $100 minimum ticket. Future liquidity never resizes the order.

The LOC cap covers estimated round-trip costs plus 2bp using only the current
bid as the prospective exit reference. This is a signal, not a future fill.
Fees: $0.0075/share, minimum $1 per side; sell 0.30bp plus $0.0002/share;
1bp adverse allowance on each side. Submit after 250ms processing plus 1s
outbound latency, also checked at 3s, before the 15:59 freeze. Exit after
15s or 30s from modeled fill ACK, plus processing and outbound latency.

SOURCE CLOCK REPAIR
The saved ARCX scheduled auction_time has two encodings: 619 selected states
encode New York 16:00 as UTC 16:00; 39 are already correct UTC. A documented
NYSE UTC bug fix supports repairing only the exact recognizable legacy
encoding. The corrected feasibility screen has 32 eligible dates, versus 5
under the preserved original UTC assumption. Neither ts_recv nor ts_event
was changed. See CLOCK_ERRATUM.txt for exact scope and limitations.

CONDITIONAL ENTRY AND EXIT EVIDENCE
Of 32 eligible orders, 22 official auction prices exceed the frozen buy cap;
10 meet the price condition. Official price eligibility does not establish
allocation, priority, auction impact, broker acceptance, or routing.

The first exit study used trade-triggered EQUS.MINI TBBO. Missing or invalid
states left both full-sample totals unresolved. It is preserved in
conditional_benchmark. We then recovered all ten price-eligible dates from
historical Alpaca SIP, not just missing or profitable paths: 114,478 quotes,
12,956,475 response bytes, complete pagination, one minute after each close.
This used existing historical-data access with no paid API or orders.

The revised exit benchmark includes the last quote strictly before each
target-1ms and every update through target+1ms, including all timestamp ties.
A successor beyond the window establishes observed coverage. Every possible
state must have valid prices, regular condition, venue identifiers and depth
for the full frozen quantity. Any invalid branch remains unknown inventory.
There is no arbitrary expiry of an unchanged standing SIP quote. Legacy SIP
sizes are converted using the historical 100-share SPY round lot, independently
verified from causal native ARCX definitions on all ten dates. These quote
envelopes do not prove the matching-engine state or physical execution.
