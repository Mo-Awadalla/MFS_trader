# SPY Accepted-Value Retest Rent to UPRO/SPXU — Critique v27

## Lineage and frozen hypothesis

Accepted-Value Retest Rent was explicitly recorded as an untouched next
hypothesis in the SPY/SH v1 report. It has not been priced with UPRO/SPXU BBO.
No futures data, margin, or short sale is used.

Compute the 09:30–09:59 SPY volume-weighted typical-price center. Opening
acceptance is bullish when both the center and 09:59 close are above the 09:30
cash open, and bearish under the symmetric inequalities.

From 10:00 through 13:29, inspect only the first SPY bar whose range touches the
opening center. The state survives if that completed bar closes back on the
accepted side and never touches the cash open. Observe one full dead minute and
cancel if it touches the cash open. Buy $100 fractional UPRO/SPXU at the
following observed ask. Exit at the observed bid one minute after SPY touches
the cash-open invalidation, or at 15:30. A failed first contact permanently
invalidates the day; no later retest substitutes.

## Institutional-interest test

**Pass, narrowly.** The center is an auction barycenter, not a rolling technical
average, and the cash open is an independently formed auction boundary. The
first return to accepted value is a direct ownership test: rejection says the
opening cohort still controls value; a cash-open traversal falsifies that
ownership. Waiting for the test can improve entry geometry without selecting a
regime from returns.

The strongest objection is that this is economically close to a VWAP pullback
and may simply delay ordinary momentum. One-minute bars obscure within-bar
order, the center is venue-volume dependent, and close-carry can remain
winner-concentrated. The rule is rejected unless independent SPY feeds agree
and winner deletion remains positive.

## Returns-blind gates

- at least two executable development entries per week;
- at least 100 entries in each development year;
- neither direction below 20%;
- at least 95% entry-quote availability;
- at least 90% cross-feed direction agreement when both signal;
- at least 80% identical action on all common overlap dates.

## Frozen return gates

PF above one; every development year positive; UPRO and SPXU positive; cash
above SPY; positive after one extra cent each fill, five extra entry minutes,
and deletion of the ten best trades; outperform sign-flipped and immediate
opening-center controls. Holdout remains locked unless all gates pass, then
requires the analogous 2024/2025, remove-best-five, SPY, cost, direction, and
consolidated-execution gates.

## Decision

Advance to returns-blind audit only. No retest count, distance, close buffer,
clock, side, or volatility filter may be changed.
