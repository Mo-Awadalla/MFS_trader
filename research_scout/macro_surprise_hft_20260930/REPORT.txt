Mechanism and fixed population
------------------------------
Test first-announced CPI content against the last strictly earlier dated public
Cleveland Fed model forecast. This is a model forecast error, not measured Wall
Street consensus surprise. It is different from the prior failed V45 ten-minute
post-release price-direction experiment, but remains part of the full exposed
research history. New independence is not claimed.

All 84 monthly releases in 2017-2023 were retained. Headline and core monthly
changes, announcement date, and 08:30 Eastern publication schedule were checked
against all 84 official archived BLS releases. All matched. The March 2017
release required the official PDF after the HTML source could not be read.
The Cleveland Fed source provides dated historical forecasts and first actual
values. Independent historical publication receipt/capture is not established.

Buy SPY if core announced minus forecast <= -0.10 percentage points and the
headline error is negative. Buy SH for core error >= +0.10 and positive headline
error. Otherwise cash. Announced values use BLS one-decimal summary precision.
The prior forecast must be no more than five calendar days old. Same-day
Cleveland Fed updates are excluded. Two holding periods: 15 and 30 seconds.

There were 33 content triggers: 19 SH and 14 SPY. Two were Good Friday exchange
holidays (14 April 2017 and 10 April 2020), so no order could be submitted. They
remain in the 84-release accounting. Signals are never moved to another day.
The two failed source requests were retained in the manifest and classified by
the known calendar, not removed according to subsequent returns.

Historical SIP quote requests cover 08:29:45-08:31:00 Eastern for every trigger.
31 successful source files contain 119,228 native quote records and 13,443,835
response bytes. Pagination completed on each. The manifest's 53,443,835-byte
figure is a conservative acquisition bound that charges each failed request
its entire 20 MB allowance; it is not measured downloaded volume.

Execution assumptions
---------------------
Baseline news decision: release +2 seconds; latest source quote strictly before
decision -1 second, and after release. Valid regular two-sided quotes with
spread <=20 bp only. A complete observed prefix is required; unchanged standing
quotes do not expire merely because they are old. Conflicting tied states are
unresolved, never selected optimistically.

Daily reset cash $10,000. Integer quantity fixed from the decision quote, at
most 10% of known ask depth, with cash buffer and $100 minimum ticket. Historical
100-share round lots for SPY and SH are still an explicit conditional assumption.
Native instrument-definition confirmation would be required before promotion.

Entry is a hypothetical guarded FOK at known ask +10 bp, rounded down to cents.
Arrival follows 250 ms processing plus one second outgoing delay. Entry ACK
adds another second; the hold begins from that ACK. Sale follows the holding
period, 250 ms processing and one second outgoing delay. Baseline entry is
08:30:03.250; exits are 08:30:20.500 and 08:30:35.500.

Quote envelopes include all tied predecessor states and every update within
one millisecond of the target, requiring a successor beyond the window. Full
quantity must fit every possible valid state. All asks above the limit imply a
price rejection; mixed or shallow states are unresolved. Exit state and full
depth must also be resolved. Before the first economic run, the CPI-specific
envelope implementation was corrected to retain all tied predecessor states;
the earlier halt module was not changed.

Costs: $0.0075/share, minimum $1 per side; sale charge 0.30 bp plus $0.0002/share;
one additional adverse basis point per side. These are assumed conservative
benchmark costs, not a claim about competition rules. Exact venue receipt,
premarket FOK support, smart routing, queue position and real fills are not
verified. A quote-reference result is conditional even when all paths resolve.
