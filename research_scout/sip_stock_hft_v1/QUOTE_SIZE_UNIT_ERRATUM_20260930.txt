The original five-trade AAPL report is not a correct implementation of its share-size threshold across 2025.

Alpaca SIP quote sizes before November 3, 2025 are round-lot counts; from that date they are shares. The original rule compared both raw representations against a minimum of 100 shares. The prior absence of signals before November must not be interpreted as lack of eligible stock events.

This remains conditional execution evidence, not a complete portfolio backtest.

The correction does not rescue this rule or establish a stock trading edge. It invalidates the original event-count interpretation and supplies stronger negative economic evidence on the correctly measured rule.
