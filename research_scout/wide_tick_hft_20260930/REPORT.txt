Scope and decision
This validates calculations under the declared public-tape queue model.
It does not establish private fills, historical account permissions, order
filters or live executability. Those limitations cannot rescue a losing model.
Close these two unchanged schedules as failed development candidates.
Do not open 2025 validation to search for a favorable period.

The next dependent experiment may use an external venue price to determine
which quotes to submit and when to cancel. It must be frozen separately before
new results, preserve cancellation latency and all partial fills, and remain
part of the same multiple-search history. No live orders were submitted.
