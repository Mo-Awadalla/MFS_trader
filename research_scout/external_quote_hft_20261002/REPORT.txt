after costs, and all twelve complete stress results have negative upper cash

Rule
Use Bybit's causal price as a predictive input to quoting on Binance. Each
side must have a price cushion exceeding the frozen 3bp maker / 6bp taker /
1bp adverse-per-execution budget. Cancel if that cushion disappears, the
external state becomes invalid or expires, retaining source, order and cancel
latency. Flatten partial inventory only after cancellations are acknowledged.
No Bybit orders or assumed Bybit fills are used. Stress fees do not reselect
which quotes are sent. Maximum inventory horizon remains below 30 seconds.

The reduction in modeled losses mainly comes with a collapse in fills.
It is not evidence of positive expected outperformance. Do not advance either
rule or spend the unopened 2025 validation set searching for a favorable year.
