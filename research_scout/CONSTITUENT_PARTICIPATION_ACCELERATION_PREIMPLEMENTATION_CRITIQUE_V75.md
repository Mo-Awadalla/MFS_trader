# Constituent Participation Acceleration: Preimplementation Critique v75

## Hypothesis

A large-cap index move driven by an unfinished basket/program order should
recruit more constituents as execution progresses.  Measure signed equal-weight
participation after 30 and 60 minutes across the repository's frozen 133-name
large-cap universe.  Trade only when the sign persists and the absolute breadth
expands in the second half-hour.

- positive expanding breadth: UPRO
- negative expanding breadth: SPXU
- otherwise: cash

Entry is 10:31 ask and exit is 15:55 bid.

This is not the prior QQQ/IWM consensus: two index proxies can agree while a few
mega-caps dominate them.  V75 observes name-level participation and requires a
within-session expansion.

## Desk critique

A professional execution desk would recognize participation broadening as a
plausible program-flow footprint.  The rule is ordinal, parameter-free, and
formed before entry.

The objections are serious:

- prices do not identify the initiating institution or remaining order;
- first-hour breadth may simply restate SPY momentum;
- the static current large-cap universe has survivorship bias;
- free IEX bars are venue-local;
- expansion can reflect independent news rather than one basket;
- any continuation may be too small for SPXU friction.

The blind audit therefore requires at least two trades/week, 100/year, balanced
directions, at least 95% complete-universe coverage, at most 80% session
selection, at most 85% action agreement with SPY first-hour momentum, at least
70% action agreement under deterministic odd/even-symbol half-universe
replications, and complete execution evidence.  No P&L is opened after a miss.

If admitted, the frozen return hurdles are PF 1.10, every year and sleeve,
adjusted-SPY outperformance, extra-cent, 10:36 delay, delete-ten, block
bootstrap, month permutation, independent execution, and mean P&L above both
final-breadth-only and SPY-momentum controls.

No threshold, universe deletion, market-cap weighting, sign inversion, UPRO-only
selection, alternate clock, or regime filter is allowed.
